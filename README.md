# Design FMEA — Automotive Disc Brake Rotor

## Overview
A Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (DFMEA) 
of an automotive disc brake rotor conducted as an 
independent quality engineering study.

## Methodology
- AIAG FMEA Reference Manual (5th Edition)
- Severity, Occurrence, Detection rating criteria
- Risk Priority Number (RPN) calculation

## Key Findings
| Failure Mode | S | O | D | RPN | Risk |
|-------------|---|---|---|-----|------|
| Thermal Cracking | 9 | 4 | 5 | 180 | High |
| Surface Corrosion | 5 | 7 | 8 | 280 | Very High |
| Excessive Wear | 7 | 6 | 4 | 168 | High |

## Key Insight
Surface corrosion recorded highest RPN of 280 — 
higher than thermal cracking despite lower severity — 
due to high occurrence in Indian climatic conditions 
and poor detection at routine service.

## India-Specific Context
Analysis contextualised for Indian operating conditions:
- Monsoon humidity and rainfall
- Salt-laden coastal air (Mumbai, Chennai, Kochi)
- Mountain terrain (Western Ghats, Himalayas)
- Vehicle overloading and poor road surfaces
